        Buddy room

        Study live with friends. You can see each other while you work, nobody gets pinged until their break, and you can share one timer so you all focus and break together.

        Rooms are peer-to-peer: there's no server of ours in between and nothing is stored. Anyone with the link can join. Others see your name, your buddy, your timer and, if you allow it, what you're working on. As on any video call, people in the room can see each other's IP address.
