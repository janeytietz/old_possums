Productive Possum: the starry vault

Your reference, made into a full-screen background (painted from a tile, then extended to widescreen with Adobe). Title top left; level, streak and tabs top right; the timer above your beast; tasks on one side and friends on the other. Readable gothic words, scribe's-hand numbers.

While you focus

Friends in your room show quietly as medallions: who's here and how far along they are. Nothing pops up.

On a break

Things held during focus come forward on the left; friends open up on the right, with paws to send and a short chat.
